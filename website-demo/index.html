01What we do

Two ways to move a vehicle.
One accountable team.

Own fleet where it counts, a nationwide carrier network where it helps. Either way, the same dispatchers answer the phone and stand behind the load.

  1. 01

    Direct carrier hauling

    Our own truck-and-trailer fleet moves new and used vehicles across the southeastern United States — dealership trades, auction purchases, port arrivals and dealer-to-dealer transfers.

  2. 02

    Nationwide brokerage

    Auto Dispatch, Inc. connects a load to hundreds of vetted third-party carriers across all 48 contiguous states — contracted lanes and one-off moves alike.

  3. 03

    Dealer & private transport

    One car or a full load. Private owners get the same dispatch team and the same condition documentation as our contract partners.

  4. 04

    Port & terminal operations

    Crews on site at port locations and terminals across the region, handling staging and vehicle movement alongside our office and shop teams.

  5. 05

    Repair & reconditioning

    Our in-house shop keeps the fleet — and the vehicles in our care — road-ready, with the work handled by H&S technicians.

02The company

A local transporter in 1999. A nationwide network today.

Since our founding in 1999, H&S Car Carriers has grown from a dedicated local transporter of used vehicles into a premier, family-owned transportation company exclusively serving the automotive industry.

We proudly partner with industry giants operating extensive routes across the southeastern United States. In 2015, we expanded our capabilities by acquiring Auto Dispatch, Inc., a brokerage firm that now services all 48 contiguous states.

That reach lets us leverage hundreds of third-party carriers to fulfill a wide range of transportation needs — from large contracted partnerships to direct dealer and private individual transactions. Based in Nahunta, Georgia, our operations are deeply rooted in our community.

04How it works

From first call to signed delivery.

No portals to learn, no anonymous load boards. A dispatcher you can reach, and a paper trail at both ends of the lane.

  1. 01

    Tell us the lane

    Call or email with pickup and delivery cities, the vehicle, and whether it runs and drives.

  2. 02

    Get a quote

    We price the move against current capacity — own truck in the Southeast, partner carrier beyond it.

  3. 03

    Load & document

    Condition is recorded at pickup, then the vehicle is loaded and secured on our trailer or an assigned carrier’s.

  4. 04

    Deliver & verify

    Delivery is confirmed against the pickup record, with proof of delivery returned to you.
